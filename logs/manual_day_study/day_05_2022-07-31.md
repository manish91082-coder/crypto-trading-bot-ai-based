## Step 5: reasoning

1. The exhaustion context (two days rejecting the highs with closes near the lows) correctly pointed **down**. But the day first ran a **stop-hunt above 24,000**, which trapped breakout buyers (B1 −1.09%).
2. **The tradeable signal was the failure of the breakout** (F1): a close above the round number, then the very next candle closing back below. It caught 1.5% net by the close, though 2.2% after the perfect top.
3. **On Sundays, round-number fake-outs appear in both directions:** Day 2 (a sweep below 10k → up) and Day 5 (a sweep above 24k → down). The **failure of a move through a round number** is the common feature.
4. A fixed level target (F1-E1, 0.53%) gave far less than the day close (1.51%). The trend-vs-range exit question from Day 3 comes up again.
