2. **H2′'s "new low of the day" filter took no trade.** On this day, "no trade" is the best possible decision, because even perfect hindsight is only ~1%.
3. **A "do-not-trade" condition is needed.** Candidate signs, all known early:

   - a first-8h range under ~1%;
   - a round number crossed many times.
   - Day 2 was also a Sunday, but its range was 3.5% after a crash, so it was not quiet. This filter must be **range / volume based, not weekday based.**
