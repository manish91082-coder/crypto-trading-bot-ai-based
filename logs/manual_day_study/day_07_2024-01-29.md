## Step 5: reasoning

1. When the price **chops around a round number for hours**, there are many small sweeps. A stop just under one sweep (−0.25%) is taken by the next, deeper one. **H2 lost once here.**
2. The real low (41,804.88) did not sweep any stronger level: the prior low of 41,620 was far below. The decisive signal was the **15:00 reclaim after the deepest sweep**, followed by the impulse.
3. **Improvement ideas to test:**
   - **(a)** For a round number that has been crossed many times that day, do not trade the first sweep. Use a sweep of the **day's low so far** (a new low of the day) plus a reclaim.
   - **(b)** Place the stop below the day's low so far, not just below the sweep candle.
   - With (a), try 2 is skipped (13:45 is not a new day low: the 00:15 low 41,874.94 was lower) and try 3 is taken (15:00 is the reclaim after the new day low at 14:45). That gives +1.256% only.
