## Step 5: reasoning

1. The direction was visible at 00:00: rising daily lows for 4 days and a close near the top of the range. The early dip held far above the prior low. Once a 15m candle closed above the prior-day high, the day was a **trend day**.
2. **Exit lesson, the opposite of Days 1–2:**
   - on this trend day the fixed 2R target left most of the move (2.9% vs 6.9% for holding to the close);
   - on Days 1–2 (range days) the fixed target was the right choice.
   - **The exit has to depend on the day type** (trend vs range). One fixed rule is wrong on one of the two.
3. The 21:17 liquidation wick (−1.5% in 1 minute, then a new high) would have stopped out a tight trailing stop. A stop below the day's structure (the day low) survived.

## Step 6: hypothesis

**H3 "breakout of the prior-day high with a long bias = trend day":**
- Long bias + a 15m close above the prior-day high → long.
- SL below the day low.
- **The exit is a trend exit** (hold to the end of the day, or trail behind a higher-timeframe structure), not a fixed 2R.
