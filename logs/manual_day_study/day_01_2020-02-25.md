## Step 5: reasoning (what made the good entry knowable)

1. The direction was knowable from the higher timeframes at 00:00: lower weekly highs, the double top at 9,990, and a heavy prior close. The day then confirmed it.
2. The best short entry (9,675) was a **retest of the prior close (9,656) from below**. The market went back to where it had closed, was rejected (wick), and fell. The prior close / open zone acted as resistance.
3. The intraday long (double-bottom breakout) was a **trap against the daily bias**. A rule that needs a closed candle above the level (R3) avoided it; an uncontrolled stop entry (R3′) lost.
4. **Exit:** the nearest known level (the prior-day low 9,473.56) gave a clean +1.37% net. Holding into the next day would have given far more (the next low was 8,633), but no rule here knew that.
