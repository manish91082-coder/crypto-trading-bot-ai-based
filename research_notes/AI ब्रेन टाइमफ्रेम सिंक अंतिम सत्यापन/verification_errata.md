## Q3. Derived calculations

### Takeaway
The arithmetic is correct. The weaknesses are omitted terms (stop slippage) and a mixing of medians with means in the stop-versus-target argument.

### Cited Findings / checks
- **0.5% × 8,000 = 40 USDT; 40 / 3% = 1,333.3 USDT notional (L317): CORRECT.** 0.085% × 1,333 = 1.13 USDT, or 2.8% of risk ("करीब 3%"): CORRECT. **Omission:** F7's own protocol assumes +1% stop slippage (F4_PROTOCOL L35). On 1,333 USDT that is ~13 USDT, so realized loss at the stop is ~53 USDT, not 40 (+33%). Stop slippage, not fees, is the dominant cost term in the risk budget.
- **Cost share of the remaining 2 ATR (L387–393): CORRECT** given 40% × typical swing %: 0.64→13.3%, 1.2→7.1%, 2.5→3.4%, 5.0→1.7%, 12→0.7%. Caveats: the swing % values are the K=3 typical **decline** sizes (SWING L49–58), labelled in the table as "आम swing". The 40% factor equals 2/5 ATR from medians, which is approximate.
- **"~2.7 years to prove Sharpe 1" (L176, L421): CORRECT.** (1.645/1.0)² = 2.71 years under IID normal annual Sharpe. The direction of the fat-tail caveat is correct.
- **Stop 3–3.5 ATR vs ~2 ATR target (L361, L433): arithmetic CORRECT.** 0.57 × 5 = 2.85 ATR back to the pivot, plus a buffer. Break-even hit rate = stop/(stop+target): 3/5 = 60% and 3.5/5.5 = 64%, before costs. Issues: (a) L433 rounds to "लगभग 3 ATR"; keep "3–3.5". (b) It mixes median quantities (5 ATR typical, ~2 ATR "आम" remaining) with a fixed-barrier argument. The conditional means of 3.0–3.8 ATR (SWING L105–108) suggest a right-skewed remaining move, so a 2 ATR fixed target forgoes the tail that the doc itself says carries the edge (L63, L311). That tension should be stated. (c) L397 treats the ~0.75 "≥1 ATR more" rate as the base rate of a "+1 ATR vs stop" barrier. The 75–78% is unconditional (no stop), so a barrier base rate with a stop at k ATR will be lower. It must be measured, not assumed.
- **70 streams = 10 coins × 7 TFs (L259):** consistent with 5m/15m/1h/4h/1D/1W/1M, but it silently drops 30m/8h/12h (see Q4).

### Inferences
- A protocol built from these numbers should lock stop slippage (≥ the F7 1%) into the per-trade risk and choose the target grid with the tail in mind, for example by including trailing-only variants with no fixed TP.

### Gaps
- No project file gives a "+k ATR before −m ATR" first-passage rate. It needs a pre-registered measurement.
